# Meshy World recovery

The original intake batch includes garage_01 (workbench), garage_02 (turntable), garage_03 (market stall) and garage_04 (Lumo house). The first two are already in the Kart production garage. The latter two are restored byte-identically from the original Git blobs into World only; they are not garage props.

The World village projection uses at most one detailed house and one market on high/ultra, when the corresponding saved facilities stream in nearby. No save format, costs, collisions, NPCs, or far LOD changed. Missing/unusable asset keeps original visual. Source materials are preserved; proportions are fitted uniformly within the existing collision envelope. Physical device frame time and rendered appearance (orientation, clipping, material fidelity) require verification. No APK or merge is claimed.
