# Design Brief — Aqua Harbor Rush

Creative direction: preserve the current Opus racing visual language. Use the hero art as the anchor and translate it into modular 3D scene construction.

World theme: bright coastal harbor with water roads and splashes
World ingredients: docks, boats, lighthouse, seaside town, bridges, water loop
Gameplay tone: fast, exciting, child-friendly, readable, premium.
Signature features: water loop, dock ramps, splash boosts, yacht-lined roadway
Hazard approach: slippery wet lanes, splash zones, dock edges, moving wave sections
Learning integration: oceans, travel, and cooperation signage integrated into route
